# v1.1.0 — 2026-07-12

This release is about editing your resume where you can see it, and getting an AI assist when the wording feels stiff.

- **Edit right on the document.** Bullets, links, and even language proficiency fields can now be edited inline, directly on the rendered resume or cover letter — no separate form to hop between.
- **Humanize AI-written sections.** A new humanizer smooths out AI-sounding phrasing in your resume or cover letter, with a preview before anything is applied.
- **See exactly where your experience falls short.** The ATS panel now shows skill-by-skill gaps between what the job asks for and what your resume shows.
- **Never lose an old draft.** A new Documents page keeps full version history for every resume and cover letter you've generated.
- **Faster job description capture.** Pasting in a job URL now pulls the description straight from the browser instead of a slower server round-trip, with a visible step-by-step progress indicator while your materials are generated.
- **Three new templates** — Compact, Two-Tone, and Academic — plus customizable page backgrounds (dots, waves, mesh, and more).
- No API key? A managed, prepaid-credit option is now available alongside bring-your-own-key providers.

**Heads up:** the older drag-and-drop job page and its `/inline` route are gone — the inline editor introduced here is now the only way to view and edit a job's materials. If you had it bookmarked, use the main job page instead.
